# Locanda accommodation presentation

Accessed: 28 September 2026

## Recommendation

Replace the three large price cards with a compact **Rooms & prices** section: one genuine Locanda bedroom photograph alongside three simple room-and-price rows. Keep the existing en-suite/air-conditioning sentence, one shared “Prices per person” label, a visible €250 non-refundable deposit note, and one “Book with Annie” button. Put the full payment schedule in an existing-style FAQ, preserving its wording and amounts.

This is a design recommendation, not a measured conversion claim. The current repeated card borders, oversized prices, repeated per-person labels and numbered payment columns give basic accommodation choices the appearance of a product pricing table. A real bedroom photograph would answer a useful question that the current section does not: what the accommodation looks like.

## Options, ranked for this brief

1. **Bedroom photograph beside a short price list — preferred.** More personal and useful without adding much text. On mobile, put the image above the list. Use only an authentic, approved Locanda bedroom photo; avoid assigning one image to a particular room type unless verified.
2. **Short price list without a photograph — best immediate fallback.** The smallest and simplest presentation; avoids introducing an unrelated venue image or repeating the pool photograph. Use thin row dividers, ordinary-sized prices and the existing site typography.
3. **Three cards with individual room photographs.** Appropriate only if each room type has a distinct, accurate photo and meaningful differences to explain. Otherwise it repeats the current structure, increases mobile scrolling and risks suggesting more room differences than are known.
